The problem

Missed doses aren't a small mistake. For elders managing chronic conditions, they're the difference between stable and hospitalized.

Non-adherence quietly drives some of the most preventable hospital readmissions among elderly patients — and most of it comes down to simply forgetting, or losing track of a schedule.

How PULSE works

One ring, one rhythm — a physical dosage cycle a caregiver can see across the room.

01

Load once a week

A caregiver or pharmacist loads the week's doses into color-coded chambers — no daily setup required.

02

Ring lights, then rings

At each scheduled time the chamber glows first, then chimes — giving a visual cue before an audible one, gentler for hearing-impaired users.

03

Dispense on a simple press

One press releases exactly that dose. No counting, no second-guessing which pill is which.

04

Family gets notified if missed

If a dose isn't taken within its window, a text goes to a designated family member or caregiver — closing the loop without needing an app open all day.
